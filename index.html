What the tools do

Ordinary back-office work, done by software instead of by hand

Most of what Varinta builds replaces something a person is doing manually today. The work is countable, so the result is too.

Pull invoices out of email

Vendor invoices arrive as PDF attachments and someone retypes them. A tool reads the inbox, pulls the fields, and writes them to a spreadsheet on your drive.

Combine locations into one view

If you run five locations of the same business, you have five versions of every report. One tool merges them so you can compare rather than guess.

Track what you buy and what you sell

Purchase cost against sale price, by item, by vendor, by month. The number you need before a price negotiation, not three weeks after it.

The difference worth knowing

These tools are hard-coded. They are not an AI wrapper.

A lot of what is being sold right now is a thin layer over a language model. Every time it runs, it sends your data to a vendor and charges you for the privilege. The answer can also change between runs, because the model is guessing.

Varinta builds deterministic tools. The rules are written in code. The same inputs produce the same outputs, every time, and you can ask exactly why a number came out the way it did.

Once a tool is delivered, running it costs you nothing. No tokens. No metered API bills. No per-seat charges. It runs on your machine or your drive, as many times as you want, for free.

Who this is for

Consumer-facing businesses under roughly $50 million in revenue — clinics, shops, service companies, distributors, multi-location operators. Usually the owner is still the person who knows where everything is, and that is exactly the bottleneck worth removing.
