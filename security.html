Security & Your Data

You keep your data. You grant the access. You take it back whenever you want.

The short version

  • Your data stays in your accounts. Varinta does not host it, copy it, or keep a warehouse of it.
  • Email access is granted by you, through Google’s own approval screen, and you can shut it off from your own account settings in about two clicks.
  • File access is you sharing one folder, exactly like sharing a folder with a coworker. You can unshare it the same way.
  • Access is always the narrowest access that makes the tool work, and nothing beyond it.
